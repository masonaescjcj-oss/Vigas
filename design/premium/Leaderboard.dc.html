§ Methodology

How the rankings are made.

I

Real sessions, not benchmarks

Win share counts how often a model finishes real agent tasks that users accept — across chat, API and RetroBuild.

II

Blind pairwise votes

In Arena, people compare two hidden models and vote. Scores use a Bradley–Terry model with 95% confidence intervals.

III

Cost is part of the score

Every ranking shows dollars per completed task, so you can see who is best for the money — not just best.
