Claude Opus 5.5 takes the top spot on agent leaderboards — by the widest margin yet

Anthropic’s new flagship wins one in five real-world agent sessions on Vigas, opening a 3.5-point lead over Gemini 4 Carbon. But the price per task tells a different story.

SMSara Moradi5 Nov 2026, 09:12 · Updated 09:40 · 6 min read
On this page
What changed The numbers Best isn’t cheapest What it means for builders FAQ

Anthropic’s new flagship now wins 20.04% of real-world agent sessions on Vigas — one in five. Across 373 million sessions, the ordering at the top of the board changed three times in a single week before settling.

The gap matters more than the rank. A 3.5-point lead over Gemini 4 Carbon is the widest the overall board has recorded, and it held across coding, research and web tasks.

What changed

Three things moved at once: longer agent horizons, cheaper serving and a wave of open-weight releases. Each alone would have reshuffled the rankings; together they compressed a year of progress into a quarter.

“Price is the last benchmark. Once a capability is cheap enough, it stops being a feature and becomes a default.”— Vigas research note, 2026

Best isn’t cheapest

At $6.82 per completed task, Opus 5.5 costs eleven times as much as Claude Fable 5.1, which wins 13.15% of sessions at $0.61. For many teams, the second number is the one that matters.

Key numbers
  • 20.04% — top agent win share this week
  • −38% — blended inference price change, quarter on quarter
  • 327 — models available on the Vigas router

What it means for builders

Keep your provider a configuration, not an architecture. Route by task, measure cost per completed task rather than per token, and revisit the board monthly — the frontier now moves faster than most release cycles.
